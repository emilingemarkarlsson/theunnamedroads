# Idea backlog (The Unnamed Roads)

Company and product ideas live as **GitHub Issues** in this repo, labeled `idea`.

## Quick links

- **Open ideas:** https://github.com/emilingemarkarlsson/theunnamedroads/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Aideas
  - (filter: `is:issue is:open label:idea`)
- **Active:** add label `idea:active`
- **Parked:** add label `idea:parked`
- **New idea:** https://github.com/emilingemarkarlsson/theunnamedroads/issues/new?template=company-idea.yml

## How to use

1. Click **New issue** → **Company / product idea**
2. Fill one-liner, problem, wedge, links
3. Weekly: open the filtered list, promote / park / close
