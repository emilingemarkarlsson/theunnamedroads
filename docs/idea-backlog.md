# Idea backlog (The Unnamed Roads)

Company and product ideas live as **GitHub Issues** in this repo, labeled `idea`.

## Quick links

- **Open ideas:** https://github.com/emilingemarkarlsson/theunnamedroads/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Aidea
- **Active only:** https://github.com/emilingemarkarlsson/theunnamedroads/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Aidea%3Aactive
- **New idea:** https://github.com/emilingemarkarlsson/theunnamedroads/issues/new?template=company-idea.yml

## How to use

1. **New issue** → **Company / product idea**
2. Fill one-liner, problem, wedge, links, next step
3. Weekly review: promote (`idea:active`), park (`idea:parked`), or close
