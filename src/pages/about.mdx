    <h1 class="text-4xl font-semibold sm:text-5xl" style="color: var(--roads-text)">One founder, a lot of AI, many companies</h1>
    <p class="text-lg" style="color: var(--roads-text-muted)">
      The Unnamed Roads is an AI-native venture studio in Stockholm, Sweden. It is a new kind of
      accelerator: instead of hiring a team for every new company, AI does almost all of the work
      (research, code, design, writing, data analysis and day-to-day operations), and one founder
      decides what is worth building.
    </p>
    <p style="color: var(--roads-text-muted)">
      The name comes from a simple idea: most obvious business ideas are already taken, but there are
      still many small, specific problems nobody has built for. AI makes it cheap enough to try them.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="section-shell space-y-4">
    <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold" style="color: var(--roads-text)">The founder: Emil Ingemar Karlsson</h2>
    <p style="color: var(--roads-text-muted)">
      Emil is an engineer and founder who builds across the stack: data platforms, AI automation and
      AI agents, and the business conversations that decide what is worth building. His view in one
      sentence: <em>AI can build almost anything now; what decides the outcome is building the right
      thing for the right user at the right price.</em>
    </p>
    <ul class="list-disc space-y-2 pl-5" style="color: var(--roads-text-muted)">
      <li>Shipped his first production AI product in July 2024: translation pipelines into all major languages using the OpenAI API.</li>
      <li>Has worked with applied AI on enterprise data platforms (Databricks, BigQuery, Azure): forecasting, machine learning and language analysis of more than 1.4 million support cases.</li>
      <li>Builds and runs production AI agent setups with scoped tools, memory, cost limits and human approval before anything ships.</li>
      <li>Builds MCP servers (the open standard that lets AI assistants use tools and data) and runs every studio project with AI coding agents.</li>
      <li>Background in hockey analytics, which became the studio&rsquo;s longest-running project, <a class="text-accent underline" href="/projects/the-hockey-analytics/">The Hockey Analytics</a>.</li>
    </ul>
    <p style="color: var(--roads-text-muted)">
      Personal site and full background: <a class="text-accent underline" href="https://emilingemarkarlsson.com/" rel="noopener">emilingemarkarlsson.com</a>.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="section-shell space-y-4">
    <h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold" style="color: var(--roads-text)">How the studio works</h2>
    <ul class="list-disc space-y-2 pl-5" style="color: var(--roads-text-muted)">
      <li><strong>Small tests first.</strong> Every idea starts as the smallest thing that can be put in front of real people.</li>
      <li><strong>AI does the work, a human decides.</strong> Nothing is published, deployed or sent to anyone outside the studio without a human yes.</li>
      <li><strong>Honest status.</strong> Every project is listed as Live, Testing or Parked. Parked projects stay visible so the lessons are not lost.</li>
      <li><strong>Few projects get full attention.</strong> Only a handful of projects are actively grown at any time; the rest wait.</li>
    </ul>
