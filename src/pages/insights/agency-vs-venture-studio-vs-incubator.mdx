# Agency vs Venture Studio vs Incubator

**Comparison page** | Part of [Insights](/insights)

Searchers comparing operating models usually want one answer: *who owns the company, who does the work, and what gets optimized?* Here is the short version used at [The Unnamed Roads](/).

## Side-by-side

| | **Agency** | **Incubator / Accelerator** | **Venture studio** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Owns the company?** | Client owns | Founders own (studio takes small equity) | Studio builds and owns significant equity |
| **Primary product** | Client delivery | Mentorship + capital + network | Ventures + shared operating system |
| **Success metric** | Billable work / retainers | Demo day, follow-on funding | Validated learning → durable ventures |
| **Team model** | Staffed for client demand | Cohort of external teams | Shared infra across portfolio bets |
| **Typical risk** | Utilization and scope creep | Dilution and cohort quality | Attention spread across too many bets |

## Where agencies win

Choose an agency when you need a bounded deliverable you will own: a site, a campaign, a integration. Agencies are optimized for **client outcomes under a SOW**, not for compounding portfolio learning.

## Where incubators / accelerators win

Choose an incubator or accelerator when you already have a founding team and need capital, mentors and peer pressure on a fixed timeline. They are optimized for **external teams**, not for building the company themselves.

## Where venture studios win

Choose a venture studio when the hard part is **originating and operating** multiple bets with shared infrastructure. Studios win when methodology, tooling and decision memory transfer across ventures.

## The AI-native twist

Most studio content still describes traditional studios with large headcount. An **AI-native venture studio** adds:

1. **Company OS** — signal → decision → work → outcome → lesson (not Notion + vibes)
2. **Focus · Monitor · Parked** — bounded attention, honest public status
3. **Human gates** — agents draft and execute inside policy; publish and consequential external actions stay approved
4. **Replaceable providers** — Linear, Vercel, models are adapters, not the domain model
