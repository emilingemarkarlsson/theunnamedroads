# AI-Native Venture Studio Operating System

**Pillar page** | Part of [Insights](/insights) · **Status: Avvecklat** (platform wound down September 2026 — kept as reference)

Most venture studios describe their stack as Notion + Slack + a CRM. That works for coordination — it does not compound learning. **TUR Company OS** was the operating system behind [The Unnamed Roads](/): a decision-first control plane where portfolio state, approval policy and organizational memory stayed canonical. The product surface is no longer active.

## The problem with dashboard-first studios

Traditional studio ops optimize for visibility: boards, backlogs, status colors. AI-native studio ops optimize for **decisions that compound**:

1. **Signal** — growth evidence, inbox, market change
2. **Decision** — what we will try, and why now
3. **Expected outcome** — falsifiable before work starts
4. **Work unit** — bounded execution with traceability
5. **Actual outcome** — measured against expectation
6. **Human correction** — what we got wrong
7. **Reviewed lesson** — durable memory, not chat noise

Agents belong **inside** steps 4–5 once the workflow is stable enough to evaluate — not as always-on employees approving their own sends.

## Three planes

| Plane | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Control** | Governance, policies, approvals, portfolio hierarchy |
| **Execution** | Humans, agents and workflows behind adapters |
| **Knowledge** | Events, decisions and lessons in Postgres — not duplicated in SaaS |

Company OS owns state, policy, audit and memory. Providers (Linear, Vercel, Supabase, model routers) stay replaceable.

## Focus · Monitor · Parked

Attention is the scarcest resource in a multi-venture studio. Full policy: [Focus · Monitor · Parked](/insights/focus-monitor-parked).

- **Focus** — at most three bets with active validation contracts and growth ranking
- **Monitor** — live ventures visible to the market but outside daily growth ranking
- **Parked** — archived or dormant; no false “Active” signals

Today’s Focus bets: [The Hockey Analytics](https://thehockeyanalytics.com/), Field Notes distribution here, and [Föräldraledighetsplaneraren](https://tur-parentalleave.vercel.app/).

Comparing models? Start here: [Agency vs venture studio vs incubator](/insights/agency-vs-venture-studio-vs-incubator).

## Approval levels (sketch)

| Level | Policy |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Read only — automatic |
| 1 | Reversible internal write — automatic within budget |
| 2 | External low-risk — standing policies |
| 3 | Consequential external — explicit mobile approval |
| 4 | Prohibited autonomous — human-only |

Publish, cold outreach and production deploy sit at Level 3 or 4. Drafts, research and internal commits can be Level 1–2 under MethodPolicy spend gates.

## What this looks like in practice

**Morning:** PostHog Focus funnels and inbox signals surface at most three mobile decisions.

**Midday:** Each Focus project keeps one active validation contract — one claim surface, one redistribution asset.

**Afternoon:** Marketing and development drafts run free-first models under spend gates; deterministic policy still owns approvals.

**Evening:** Approved drafts can commit, deploy and IndexNow after policy. Outcomes return to growth memory.
