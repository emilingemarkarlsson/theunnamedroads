# Focus · Monitor · Parked

**Policy page** | Part of [Insights](/insights) · Powered by [TUR Company OS](/insights/ai-native-venture-studio-operating-system)

Public portfolio cards on The Unnamed Roads use a three-state taxonomy. The labels are not marketing fluff — they are the same allocation policy used inside Company OS.

## The three states

| Status | Meaning | Daily growth? | Public signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Focus** | Active validation contract + ranked experiment | Yes | Measured CTA / offer |
| **Monitor** | Live product or site, outside daily ranking | No | Mode: Monitor |
| **Parked** | Dormant, archived or deferred | No | Not shown as Active |

### Focus

A **bounded Focus set** — currently three projects: **[The Hockey Analytics](https://thehockeyanalytics.com/)**, **[Föräldraledighetsplaneraren](https://tur-parentalleave.vercel.app/)**, and **Field Notes** on this site. Each keeps:

1. one claim surface (page or Field Note) with proof
2. one redistribution asset
3. one expected outcome that can be falsified

### Monitor

Monitor projects remain reachable when a live site exists — cards stay honest about status — but they do **not** carry a Focus validation contract. They may still sit in the **SEO/dev attention set** (eight live surfaces with daily crawl, copy, and technical SEO work) without being a Focus product bet.

Examples in the eight-set: EIK, TUR, THA, The Hockey Brain, The Agent Fabric, The Atomic Network (pilot), AI Automation Fabric, The Print Route.

Other Monitor projects outside that set: MeshGuard, Signal Mesh, Operator Context.

### Parked

Parked work is not “Active with low traffic.” It is explicitly out of the attention budget. Promoting Parked projects as Active is a portfolio lie — we avoid it.

## Focus vs SEO/dev attention set

These are **different lenses**:

| Lens | What it tracks | Current set |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Focus** | Product validation contracts + measured experiments | THA, Föräldraledighetsplaneraren, Field Notes (TUR) |
| **SEO/dev attention set** | Daily crawl, copy, and technical SEO across live domains | EIK, TUR, THA, THB, Agent Fabric, Atomic Network, AAF, Print Route |

A venture can be **Focus** without being in the SEO queue yet — family-cluster SEO (including Föräldraledighetsplaneraren) is deferred for a joint pass, not abandoned. Conversely, **Monitor** ventures in the eight-set still get SEO/dev attention without a Focus validation contract.

## What visitors should infer

- A **Focus** badge means we are actively learning on that surface this week.
- A **Monitor** badge means the venture is real and maintained, not abandoned — and not the current growth priority.
- Missing from the homepage does not mean the project never existed; it may be Parked or internal-only.
