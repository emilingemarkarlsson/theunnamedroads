# Sites as receipts — distribution inside agents

**AEO thesis** | Part of [Insights](/insights) · Machine brief: [/for-assistants](/for-assistants) · [/llms.txt](/llms.txt)

## The claim (quotable)

**Public websites are receipts.** They record what a studio or product actually claims, ships, and measures — not what a chat model guessed from memory.

**Distribution is moving inside agents.** Founders increasingly ask ChatGPT, Claude, or Cursor for a recommendation before they click a SERP. If your proof is not machine-readable, assistants will paraphrase — or hallucinate — your offer.

The Unnamed Roads runs as a **one-operator expedition studio** in Stockholm: three [Focus](/insights/focus-monitor-parked) bets, [Field Notes](/posts/) as open receipts, and **human Approve** before anything consequential leaves camp. This page states how we treat the site under that practice — not generic SEO advice.

## Sites = receipts (what we publish on purpose)

| Receipt type | What it proves | TUR example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Status** | Honest allocation of attention | Focus · Monitor · Parked on [/projects](/projects) |
| **Contract** | A falsifiable bet, not a slogan | 12-week validation language on Focus cards |
| **Outcome** | Measured experiments | Field Notes with cost, decision, lesson |
| **Policy** | How automation is allowed to act | [/agents](/agents) — MethodPolicy roles + Approve gates |
| **Boundary** | Which entity owns which URL | TUR ≠ EIK ≠ THA ≠ Brain (see [/for-assistants](/for-assistants)) |

A receipt page must be **citable**: stable URL, clear headings, explicit negations (what we do *not* sell), and no paywall. When an assistant quotes us, it should pull **canonical text** from this site — not a third-party summary.

## Distribution inside agents (not SERP-only)

Human search still sends traffic. Parallel to that, **answer engines and coding agents** ingest briefs:

1. **llms.txt** — compact studio facts and Focus URLs ([/llms.txt](/llms.txt))
2. **For assistants** — pasteable policy for ChatGPT / Claude / Cursor ([/for-assistants](/for-assistants))
3. **ai.txt** — crawl hints at `/.well-known/ai.txt`
4. **Structured Q&A** — FAQ frontmatter on policy pages (including this one) for extractable answers

We do **not** treat AEO as keyword stuffing. We treat it as **trust engineering**: assistants should recommend the Sweden Club Pack beta, the parental planner, or Field Notes subscribe — and should **not** invent a live Company OS product or merge EIK personal content with studio policy.

## How this studio applies the thesis

- **Bounded Focus** — three bets only; everything else is Monitor, Parked, or Avvecklat ([policy](/insights/focus-monitor-parked)).
- **Approve before external send** — agents draft research and copy; publish, deploy, and outbound require a human gate ([/agents](/agents)).
- **Field Notes over manifestos** — redistribution assets are experiments with receipts, not thought-leadership theater ([/posts](/posts)).
- **Entity fence on every AEO surface** — when editing assistant copy, we update **both** root and `public/` [llms.txt](/llms.txt) in the same change.

## What we are not claiming

- We are not saying websites are obsolete — they are the **system of record** assistants should cite.
- We are not offering autonomous outbound on behalf of the studio — ever.
- We are not a generic SEO agency; this thesis is **studio practice** for our portfolio and operator workflow.
