**One login for every brand a garden-equipment workshop services.**

## What it is

A hub for workshops that repair garden and outdoor power equipment from many brands. Service bulletins, training, manuals, exploded views and spare parts in one place instead of one portal per brand.

## Who it is for

Dealers and service workshops for garden and outdoor power equipment.

## How AI does the work

Research, the website and the prototype were built with AI coding assistants by one person.

## Status: Testing

Testing. A website is live on a temporary address while demand is validated.
