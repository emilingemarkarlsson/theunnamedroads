**Fixed-price data platforms and analytics agents that ship.**

## What it is

A fixed-price offer for companies that want a modern data platform and AI analytics agents without a long consulting project.

## Who it is for

Small and mid-sized companies that need analytics but do not have a data team.

## How AI does the work

Offer design, the website and most of the delivery work are produced with AI agents, reviewed by a human with data and analytics experience.

## Status: Testing

Testing. The website is being prepared and is not public yet.
