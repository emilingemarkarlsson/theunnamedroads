## The decision

Should a solo AI venture studio optimize every live domain for search — or
concentrate organic visibility where a validation contract already exists?

We chose concentration.

## What we run now

Exactly **three Focus projects**:

1. **The Hockey Analytics** — Sweden Club Pack / league intelligence for club leaders
2. **The Unnamed Roads** — Field Notes for indie founders (this channel)
3. **Föräldraledighetsplaneraren** — Swedish parental-leave plans via SEO/AEO

Everything else is **Monitor** (measured, no daily growth execution) or **Parked**.

## The AI-era distribution pack (v0)

For one Focus project per experiment we ship:

1. one canonical claim surface (page or Field Note) with proof + FAQ answers
2. one redistribution asset (LinkedIn / community post)
3. instrumented `primary_cta_clicked` + conversion event **before** publish
4. a separate human Approve for any external send

No autonomous blog spray across ten domains. No MCP mesh “for visibility”
until a Focus offer has a stable tool contract worth exposing.

## Expected outcome (14 days)

For this Field Notes contract: ten relevant subscribers **or** three qualified
replies asking for a playbook, implementation detail, or advisory next step.

If neither appears after two clearly different evidence-based notes, we stop
and revise the hypothesis — we do not add more channels.
