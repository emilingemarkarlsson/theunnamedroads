## Problem
Public proof is studio-owned (portfolio, Field Notes, stack costs). External operators comparing models need **third-party or partner evidence** that Company OS-driven execution produces outcomes.

## Goal
Ship **3 case studies** within two quarters that prove Focus allocation + human gates + measured learning — without inventing metrics.

## Non-goals
- Paid testimonials without evidence
- Agency-style client logos without permission
- Automated case-study generation from chat logs

## Case study template

1. **Context** — who / which vertical / starting constraint
2. **Decision** — what Focus bet was chosen and why
3. **System** — Company OS artifacts used (validation contract, approval gate, status)
4. **Work** — what shipped in ≤12 weeks
5. **Outcome** — measured result (PostHog, revenue, waitlist, retention) with source
6. **Lesson** — human correction retained
